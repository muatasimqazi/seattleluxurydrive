
# Project Overview

## Project Name

Seattle Luxury Drive (SJD Luxury Car Rentals)

---

## Mission

Provide premium executive transportation, luxury chauffeur services, airport transfers, and curated luxury vehicle experiences throughout the Greater Seattle Area.

The company is positioned as a luxury transportation and concierge service rather than a traditional rental car business.

---

## Business Model

Seattle Luxury Drive operates using a concierge-first reservation model.

Customer Journey:

1. Customer explores the website.
2. Customer submits a booking request.
3. Concierge team reviews the request.
4. Concierge team contacts the customer.
5. Reservation details are finalized manually.
6. Payment is collected offline.
7. Service is fulfilled.

This workflow prioritizes personalized service and flexibility over automation.

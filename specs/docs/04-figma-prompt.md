### Phase 9 — Booking Request Page

Create a premium 3-step booking form experience.

Page header:

- Headline: Request a Reservation
- Subheadline: Complete the form below and a member of our concierge team will contact you within 4 business hours.
- Progress indicator (3 labeled steps, gold for active/completed)

Step 1 — Your Trip:

- Service Type (radio cards: Self Drive / With Chauffeur)
- Vehicle selector (card display with vehicle image and price)
- Rental Type (radio: Hourly / Full Day / Multi-Day)
- Date and time pickers
- Pickup and dropoff location inputs

Step 2 — Your Details:

- First Name, Last Name
- Email Address, Phone Number
- Preferred Contact Method (radio: Phone Call / Text / Email)

Step 3 — Review & Submit:

- Occasion (optional dropdown)
- Special Requests (optional textarea with character counter)
- Review summary card (all Step 1 and 2 values)
- Privacy Policy consent checkbox
- Submit button: "Send Request"

Design all form states:

- Default (empty)
- Focused (gold border)
- Filled
- Validation error (red border + inline error message)
- Loading (spinner on submit button, fields disabled)
- Submission error (red error banner above form)

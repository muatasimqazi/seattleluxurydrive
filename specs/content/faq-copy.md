## Booking & Reservations

### How do I make a reservation?

Simply submit a booking request through our website. A member of our concierge team will review your request, contact you to discuss details, and finalize your reservation.

### How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weekends, corporate events, weddings, and peak travel periods. However, we will do our best to accommodate last-minute requests whenever availability permits.

### Do you require a deposit?

Deposit and payment requirements vary based on the reservation, vehicle, and service requested. Our team will provide details during the reservation process.

---

## Chauffeur Services

### Do you offer chauffeur services?

Yes. Chauffeur service is available for executive transportation, airport transfers, special occasions, corporate events, and other luxury transportation needs.

### Can I request a custom itinerary?

Absolutely. Our concierge team can help coordinate customized transportation arrangements based on your schedule and requirements.

---

## Vehicle Rentals

### Can I drive the vehicle myself?

Self-drive and chauffeur options are available depending on the reservation. Availability is determined based on the vehicle, itinerary, and reservation details.

### What vehicles do you currently offer?

Our launch fleet features a 2021 Rolls-Royce, with additional luxury vehicles planned as part of our future expansion.

### Is chauffeur service required?

Not always. Depending on the reservation, customers may choose between self-drive and chauffeur service options.

## Airport Transfers

### Do you provide airport transportation?

Yes. We offer luxury airport transfers to and from Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SeaTac) and destinations throughout the Greater Seattle Area.

### Do you monitor flight arrivals?

When flight information is provided, our team can coordinate transportation based on your arrival schedule.

## Service Area

### What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Mercer Island, Lynnwood, Everett, Edmonds, Mukilteo, Tacoma, SeaTac Airport, and surrounding communities throughout the Greater Seattle Area.

### Can transportation be arranged outside the Greater Seattle Area?

Depending on the reservation, custom transportation arrangements may be available. Please contact our team to discuss your specific requirements.

## Special Events

### Do you provide transportation for weddings and celebrations?

Yes. We offer luxury transportation for weddings, anniversaries, milestone celebrations, formal events, and other special occasions.

## Pricing

### How much does a reservation cost?

Pricing starts at $350 per hour. Final pricing varies based on vehicle selection, reservation duration, service type, destination, and other requirements.

### Do you offer custom pricing?

Yes. Custom quotes are available for airport transfers, corporate events, weddings, special occasions, and extended reservations.

## Contact

### How can I contact Seattle Luxury Drive?

You can submit a booking request online, complete our contact form, call us directly, or email our team. We will respond as quickly as possible.

### How quickly will someone respond to my inquiry?

Our goal is to respond to all booking and contact requests within 4 business hours during normal business hours.



# Brand Voice

## Purpose

This document defines the voice, tone, language standards, and messaging principles for Seattle Luxury Drive.

All website copy, marketing materials, emails, advertisements, social media content, and customer communications should follow these guidelines.

---

# Brand Personality

Seattle Luxury Drive should be perceived as:

- Professional
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Welcoming
- Sophisticated
- Discreet
- Reliable
- Concierge-Oriented

The brand should feel more like a luxury hotel concierge or private transportation service than a traditional car rental company.

---

# Voice Attributes

## Professional

Communicate clearly and confidently.

Avoid slang and overly casual language.

Example:

"Our concierge team will contact you shortly to finalize your reservation details."

---

## Premium

Use refined language that reflects quality and attention to detail.

Example:

"Experience luxury transportation tailored to your schedule and preferences."

---

## Approachable

Luxury should never feel intimidating.

Write in a way that feels welcoming and helpful.

Example:

"We're happy to help coordinate transportation for your upcoming event."

---

## Concierge-Focused

Emphasize service and personalization.

Example:

"Every reservation is handled with personalized attention and care."

# Words To Avoid

Avoid language commonly associated with exotic car rental businesses.

Do Not Use:

- Supercar
- Exotic Car
- Fast
- Race
- Adrenaline
- Thrill Ride
- Cheapest
- Discount
- Budget
- Low Cost
- Best Deal

---

# Writing Style Guidelines

## Keep Sentences Clear

Prefer:

"Luxury transportation throughout the Greater Seattle Area."

Over:

"We proudly endeavor to provide an unparalleled transportation solution."

---

## Be Specific

Mention actual services whenever possible.

Prefer:

"Airport transfers, chauffeur services, and executive transportation."

Over:

"Transportation solutions for every need."

---

## Emphasize Service Over Vehicles

Focus on the customer experience first.

Vehicle features should support the story, not become the story.

Preferred:

"Arrive comfortably and confidently."

Less Preferred:

"Drive an expensive luxury vehicle."

---

# CTA Guidelines

Preferred CTAs:

- Request Reservation
- Contact Us
- Explore Fleet
- Speak With Our Concierge Team
- Request Airport Transfer
- Request Chauffeur Service

Avoid:

- Book Now!!!
- Get A Deal
- Limited Time Offer
- Rent Today

---

# Customer Communication Standards

Every customer interaction should communicate:

- Respect
- Professionalism
- Responsiveness
- Attention to detail

The customer should feel that their transportation experience is being personally managed rather than automatically processed.

---

# Brand Voice Summary

Seattle Luxury Drive speaks like a premium concierge service.

We are:

- Professional
- Refined
- Helpful
- Trustworthy
- Service-Oriented

We are not:

- Flashy
- Aggressive
- Salesy
- Price-focused
- Exotic-car focused

Every piece of communication should reinforce confidence, luxury, and personalized service.

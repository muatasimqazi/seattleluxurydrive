

# Testimonials Strategy

## Purpose

Testimonials are used to:

- Build trust
- Increase booking conversions
- Reinforce premium positioning
- Improve local SEO credibility

Testimonials should appear on:

- Homepage
- Vehicle Detail Pages
- Booking Request Page
- Future SEO Landing Pages

---

# Testimonial Sources

## Launch Phase

Primary Source:

- Google Reviews

Secondary Sources:

- Facebook Reviews
- Direct Customer Feedback

Preferred hierarchy:

1. Google Reviews
2. Facebook Reviews
3. Manual Customer Testimonials

Reason:

Google Reviews provide the highest level of trust and credibility.

# Launch Requirements

## Testimonials Are Not Required To Launch

The testimonials section is optional at launch.

If real testimonials are unavailable when the site goes live:

- Hide the testimonials section entirely in production
- Do not display placeholder testimonials to real users under any circumstances
- Add the section once a minimum of 3 real reviews are collected and confirmed

Placeholder testimonials are permitted only in Figma design mocks and local development environments. They must never appear in a production deployment.

## When Testimonials Are Ready For Production

Minimum requirements before enabling the section in production:

- 3 real testimonials collected
- Customer permission obtained for any name displayed
- All placeholder content removed from code

Preferred requirements:

- 5+ Google Reviews
- Average rating of 4.8 or higher
- At least one testimonial per category:
  - Executive Transportation
  - Airport Transfers
  - Chauffeur Service
